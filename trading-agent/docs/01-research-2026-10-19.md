# Market research — 2026-10-19

## Read this first: evidence quality

- **No primary source was opened.** In the build environment every direct page fetch failed at DNS (CoinGecko, DefiLlama, TypeSafe docs, news sites). Web *search* worked, so every figure below comes from secondary reporting surfaced by search, dated as reported.
- Labels used throughout:
  - **[REPORTED]**: a number stated by a named secondary source on the given date.
  - **[CONFLICT]**: sources disagree; a range is shown.
  - **[ESTIMATE]**: my own arithmetic or judgment.
  - **[SPECULATION]**: a hypothesis with no confirming evidence yet.
- No metric is invented. Where a number I would need is missing (current HYPE/ASTER/ENA prices, FDVs, active users, holder concentration), the cell says **MISSING**, not a guess.
- **Before any schema leaves shadow mode**, each MISSING or REPORTED cell must be re-filled from the primary source listed in the last section.

## 2. Candidates (5, not 5–10)

I found five candidates whose thesis I can trace to a dated source. Padding the list to ten would mean inventing conviction. **None qualifies as a "finalist" yet.** All are `status: candidate` in `config/finalists.json`: shadow and paper only.

### 2.1 BTC-PERP — `btc-macro-range` (bias: none)

- **Why it's here:** it's the liquidity anchor. If the loop can't show net-of-cost skill on BTC, it has none on illiquid alts. This is the **control**, not an asymmetric bet.
- **Catalyst:** FOMC Oct 27–28 (date confirmed; outcome unknown).
- **Bear case:**
  - A hike plus hawkish guidance breaks $80k.
  - ETF outflows persist.
  - Stablecoin supply contracts.
- **Invalidation:**
  - Daily close below $78k.
  - Stablecoin supply falls more than 2% over 14 days.
  - Persistently negative funding with rising open interest.
- **Value accrual:** n/a.

### 2.2 HYPE-PERP — `hype-buyback` (bias: long)

**Supply:**
- Contributor unlocks reported at ~9.9M HYPE/month through late 2027 [REPORTED, mid-2026 data, dated].
- The team reportedly cut some team allocations sharply; the Feb figure was ~140k HYPE [REPORTED].
- **Current schedule: MISSING.**

**Revenue and fees:**
- Among the top three protocols by holder revenue on DefiLlama (page dated Oct 18) [REPORTED].
- About 97–99% of perp fees go to the Assistance Fund, which buys HYPE [CONFLICT on the exact %].

**Price:** about $69.8 in July 2026; all-time high $83.27 on Aug 24 [REPORTED]. **October price: MISSING.**

**TVL, active users, holder concentration: MISSING.**

**Does value accrue to the token?** Yes, mechanically. This is the strongest value-accrual case in the set.

**Confirmed catalyst:** continuous, fee-funded buybacks.

**Speculative catalyst:** volatility spikes raise volume, and therefore buybacks [SPECULATION].

**Bear case:**
- Buyback capacity of $60–80M/month vs. unlocks worth more [REPORTED].
- Fees are pro-cyclical: in a quiet, risk-off tape the buyback shrinks exactly when you need it.
- Perp-DEX competition, e.g. Aster.
- Regulatory exposure of an offshore perps venue.
- **This is the consensus "real yield" long, so likely crowded and priced in.**

**Invalidation:**
- 30-day holder revenue down more than 50% vs. the prior 30 days.
- Contributor-wallet inflows to exchanges around unlocks.
- Sustained loss of volume share.

### 2.3 UNI-PERP — `uni-fee-switch` (bias: none, watch-list)

**Supply:**
- A 100M UNI retroactive burn (Jan 2026) [REPORTED].
- 20M UNI/year growth-budget issuance [REPORTED].

**Revenue:**
- ~$23M cumulative since the fee switch went live (Dec 2025).
- ~$4.9M per 30 days.
- $26–58M annualized [CONFLICT].
- 30-day holder revenue $8.87M on DefiLlama [REPORTED, Oct 18].

**Price:** $3.43 on Aug 12, 2026; about $3.68 undated [REPORTED]. **October price, FDV, active users: MISSING.**

**Does value accrue?** Yes, now. But the magnitude is small relative to valuation [ESTIMATE, pending FDV], and the price did not re-rate on activation. That is evidence the market sees the same thing.

**Bear case:**
- Issuance can offset the burn.
- Revenue is cyclical with DEX volume.

**Invalidation:**
- Holder revenue falls below the value of the issuance.
- Governance redirects fees.

### 2.4 ENA-PERP — `ena-trigger` (bias: none)

**Catalyst [SPECULATION]:**
- The fee switch was reportedly approved in Sept 2026.
- But ENA buybacks only start once USDe supply reaches $7.5B. USDe was ~$4.98B on Oct 7 [REPORTED], so supply must grow about 50%.

**Split mechanics:** sources disagree. One says "95% of net revenue"; another describes a stepped 5/10/15% [CONFLICT].

**Bear case:**
- USDe yield depends on funding. Funding is low, which compresses yield and historically shrank supply: a peak of $14.7B (Oct 2025) fell 56% by Dec 2025 [REPORTED].
- Scheduled unlocks around Nov 1–5; date and size conflict across sources.
- The trigger may never be hit.

**Invalidation:**
- USDe falls below $4B.
- Governance changes the trigger or the split.

### 2.5 ASTER-PERP — `aster-unlock` (bias: short)

**Supply:** ~78.4M ASTER unlocking Nov 17, reportedly ~3.9% of market cap [REPORTED, single source; verify].

**Bear case for the short:**
- Unlock shorts are a crowded, well-known trade, so likely priced in.
- Crowded shorts mean negative funding, so you pay carry, plus squeeze risk.
- Recipients may not sell.

**Invalidation:**
- The unlock is postponed or restructured.
- Funding below −30% annualized.
- Price rises through the unlock window on rising open interest.

**Revenue, TVL, users, holder concentration: MISSING.** I can't assess value accrual.
